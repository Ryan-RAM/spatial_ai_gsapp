Frostroot: The Ramble

23 January 2016, the night of the record blizzard. Something made of roots wakes up in the Ramble. You design the park for the storm night: who gets out?

How to read this

This is the Ramble in Central Park on the night of the January 2016 blizzard: real paths, terrain, trees and hour-by-hour weather. Two survivors try to reach Fifth Avenue. Something made of roots hunts them, and the cold works against them too.

  • Warm light means safety: lamps, flashlights, warming shelters.
  • The 3D view follows a survivor; the map below shows what each agent knows, their tracks, landmarks and plans.
  • The timeline is the real storm: click an hour to start the next round then.
  • Keys: Space play/pause · +/− speed · C camera · 1/2 follow · K what the agent knows · N next round
