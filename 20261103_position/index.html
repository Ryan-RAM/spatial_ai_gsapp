    Log

      How position and movement are read

      Prepositions of place and movement, turned into rules on the grid and checked after every tick (src/sim/relations.js; thresholds in config.relations). In the diagrams the warm dot is you, red is the creature, the cyan diamond is the vaccine case and yellow is the relation itself. Sight still uses the Euclidean distance (a 120° cone, blocked by solids) and hearing the acoustic distance (muffled by what is in between) from the previous assignment.

      Position and movement

      Position

      In a tree (above / below)

      Rule. Press Space next to a tree to climb it. Up there you are above the ground: it cannot see you or reach you. It is "below" when it stands within 2 cells of your tree.

      Shown as. Double rings on your tree with an ▲ IN THE TREE tag on the minimap, a ▼ BELOW tag on the creature, and the IN A TREE chip.

      Position

      Behind

      Rule. Within 9 cells (the fog), and more than 90° from where the other faces. Its cone is 120° wide, so behind it you are never seen.

      Shown as. The rear half-disc is shaded on the minimap and in 3D, with a BEHIND tag.

      Position

      Adjacent

      Rule. No occupiable cell in between: one walking step in 8 directions, without cutting the corner of a tree, bush or rock.

      Shown as. One yellow outline around both cells. Checked between you and the creature (that is how it gets you), the case, the hospital door and each crow’s tree.

      Position

      Between

      Rule. On the plan, the creature projects inside the segment from you to your goal and lies within 1.6 cells of it. Goal: the case, then the hospital door.

      Shown as. A dashed line from you to the goal, crossed out at the creature, with an IN THE WAY tag.

      Movement

      Toward / away from

      Rule. After each tick, the mover’s path distance to where the other one stood is compared with before.

      Shown as. An arrow on the minimap from the old cell to the new: solid when closing in, dashed when opening up.

      Movement

      Up / down

      Rule. You got into a tree, or out of it, in this tick. You can hold on for 7.5 seconds, then the cold makes you climb down.

      Shown as. An ↑ UP or ↓ DOWN tag rises or sinks and fades; an arrow cone in 3D; a bar shows how long you can hold on.

      Movement

      Around

      Rule. While you stay within 8 cells of the case, the turning of your bearing from it is added up. At 270° you went around.

      Shown as. The trail is drawn as a thick line with an arrowhead and an AROUND tag, then fades.

      Movement

      Past

      Rule. The path distance between you falls, bottoms out at 4 cells or less and grows again, while the creature never saw you.

      Shown as. A ring at the closest point with a » PAST tag, then it fades.

      Behaviours triggered by position

      Behaviour · intrinsic capability

      Crows warn

      Rule. Three crows perch in trees. When you are adjacent to their tree, the crow caws (then rests 6 seconds). The creature hears it if the acoustic distance is 16 or less, and comes to the crow.

      Shown as. Crows on the minimap and in 3D; their eyes light up and a dashed frame appears while you are next to them; purple rings for the caw.

      Behaviour · placement

      Ambush

      Rule. When the case’s alarm stops (6 s after you take it) and the creature has lost you, it goes to the cell two thirds of the way along your shortest walk to the hospital door: between you and the door.

      Shown as. Its mode reads "Cutting you off"; once you see it there, the IN THE WAY line appears.

      Behaviour · placement

      Hiding up a tree

      Rule. While you are in a tree it never sees you and cannot catch you, even standing below. But the case’s alarm still gives you away, and you can only hold on for 7.5 seconds.

      Shown as. The IN A TREE chip, a bar for how long you can hold on, your figure up among the branches.
